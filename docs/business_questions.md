# Business Questions

## Executive Performance

1. What is total warehouse throughput by month?
2. What is the overall picking accuracy rate?
3. What percentage of activity is completed within SLA?
4. Are warehouse teams meeting their order targets?

## Shift Performance

5. Which shift has the highest units picked per labour hour?
6. Which shift has the highest picking error rate?
7. Which shift relies most heavily on overtime?
8. Does SLA compliance vary significantly by shift?

## Workforce Analytics

9. Do trained employees show higher productivity than untrained employees?
10. Does employee experience correlate with lower error rates?
11. Which teams consistently outperform or underperform targets?

## Bottleneck Analysis

12. Which warehouse zones have the longest processing times?
13. Which product categories generate the most errors?
14. Do high-volume periods reduce accuracy or SLA compliance?
15. Which days or months show recurring operational bottlenecks?
